About Us



Welcome to SunLight PlayHouse, your destination for world-class movie experiences. We combine the magic of cinema with the comfort of modern theaters, offering you unforgettable moments with family and friends.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality entertainment, top-notch facilities, and superior customer service that bring joy and connection to every visitor.

Our Vision

To become the leading entertainment hub where stories come alive and communities connect through the love of cinema.

Our Journey

Founded in 2020, SunLight PlayHouse has grown from a single screen to a multi-experience playhouse, hosting blockbuster movies, local productions, and special screenings that bring culture and entertainment together.

Why Choose Us?

  • Comfortable seating and world-class sound systems
  • Affordable ticket pricing and special offers
  • Friendly staff and customer-first service
  • Online booking for quick & hassle-free experience
